About The Anemia Detection System

Automated multi-class diagnosis of anemia from peripheral blood smear images and complete blood count parameters using deep transfer learning.

System Architecture & Pipeline

1. Image Acquisition

Microscopic peripheral blood smear images taken under 100x oil immersion magnification.

2. Smear Validation

Validation, RGB conversion, and dynamic field inspection for blood cell analysis.

3. Hosted Cloud Inference

Roboflow hosted inference model (tri-blood-iw3al/2) with automated cell morphology detection.

4. CBC Corroboration & PDF

Cross-validation against clinical CBC indices and automated generation of PDF laboratory reports.

Clinical Purpose

Designed to assist laboratory professionals and medical practitioners in triaging peripheral blood smear samples, expediting diagnosis for iron deficiency, sickle cell disease, thalassemia, megaloblastic anemia, and dimorphic presentations.

Automated Reporting

Generates standardized, downloadable hematology PDF reports detailing primary classification, multi-class confidence distribution, and morphological evaluation markers for clinical correlation.
